# Quest test checklist

One pass in the headset, about 15 minutes. Note anything that feels wrong; a short phrase is enough.

## Get in

1. On your phone or computer, open https://shadowfolk.vercel.app/quest. It should open the game in the headset browser. If it does not, type `shadowfolk.vercel.app` in the Quest Browser.
2. Pinch **Enter Shadowfolk**. Sit down first.

## Check

| # | What to do | What should happen |
|---|------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Lift one hand | A soft, sharp-edged hand shadow on the paper within a second |
| 2 | Look at the paper, then around | It sits straight ahead at eye level. Around you: stars, a moon, pines, red curtains, lanterns, fireflies |
| 3 | Look down | A tray with three brass buttons: Book, Gentle, Skip |
| 4 | Starfish, Hare, Crocodile | Each wakes within a minute; a gold eye opens on it |
| 5 | Wait on a shape without trying | After about 6 s a gold hand appears **on your own wrist**, showing the pose |
| 6 | Dog | Thumb up, split between middle and ring fingers. Can you make it? |
| 7 | Poke **Gentle**, then try a two-hand shape with one hand | One hand is enough in Gentle mode |
| 8 | Poke **Skip** until "The Rain" | Rain falls from a cloud; your hand and arm shadow is a slide |
| 9 | Rain: hold still with no drops landing for 12 s | A gold hand shows a slide that works |
| 10 | Fireflies | They move away from your shadow; you can push them into the jar |
| 10b | Ink Wolf battle (after the puzzles) | Make any creature from your Book: it flies at the wolf. When its eyes glow red, make the Starfish to block. Is it fun? Too hard or too easy? |
| 11 | Listen | Soft music and fire; a chime when a creature wakes |
| 12 | Whole session | Smooth motion, no stutter. If it stutters, note when |
| 13 | Take the headset off and on | The game pauses and resumes |

## Send back

- Any row that failed, with one line on what you saw.
- Which shapes felt too hard or too easy.
- A screen recording if something looks wrong (Meta button, Camera, Record video).
